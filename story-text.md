# Region Forge story text (rewritten)

Words in brackets are filled in by each region: [team] is the villain team, [boss] its leader, [place] a landmark, [trade] a local job, [wild area] a forest, river, lake and so on. Each region picks one line from each group.

## Mining and pumping

Team names: The Deepcut, Hollow Works, The Seam, Bracken Extraction, The Undercut

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Pump the water out from under [place] and sell it to the cities.
- Reopen the old mine under [place], even though wild Pokémon live in it now.
- Dig up the [wild area] for the stone underneath, before the League can protect it.

**Method:**
- They bought their digging permits from an official who then left the region.
- They run their machines at night, so nobody sees the digging.
- They got local families to sign leases without explaining what the leases allowed.

**After badge 1:**
- A [trade] shows you her well. The water has turned brown since [team] started digging nearby.
- [team] workers are marking out farmland near [place] for a mine. The farmers say they never sold it.
- The [trade]s vote on leasing their land to [team]. The vote is close, and it passes.

**After badge 2:**
- [team] pumps run all night near [place]. By morning, the stream there is half empty.
- The river by [place] has dropped so low that the Water Pokémon are moving away.

**After badge 3:**
- You find [team]'s digging plans. They mean to dig under [place] next.
- A [team] foreman offers you a job. When you say no, he tells you to stay off his site.
- You find a letter signed by [boss]. Now you know who runs [team].

**After badge 5:**
- The [trade]s stop working for [team], so [team] brings in workers from other towns.
- A [team] tunnel caves in. Rangers spend the day digging out the wild Pokémon trapped inside.

**After badge 6:**
- [boss] offers you money to stop getting in the way. You turn it down.
- [team] floods its own tunnels to hide what it has been digging up.

**After badge 8:**
- [boss] has started the biggest dig yet, at [place]. Stop [team] there and the digging ends.
- Every [team] crew in the region heads to [place] for one last dig. You head there too.

## Closing the region to outsiders

Team names: The Quiet Hand, Vigil, The Warden Circle, Thornguard, The Standing Watch

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Close [place] to outsiders for good, by force if the League will not do it.
- Pull up the region's new roads so the wild places go back to how they were.
- Turn every trainer away at the border until the [wild area] has recovered.

**Method:**
- They put up roadblocks without permission and move them every few days.
- Some gym leaders secretly support them and lose to their members on purpose.
- They keep their membership secret, so nobody knows who to trust.

**After badge 1:**
- A fence now blocks a path you used last week. The sign on it is signed by [team].
- [team] members turn two trainers away at [place]. They say the area is closed to visitors.
- A [trade] tells you the [wild area] was healthier before trainers started coming through.

**After badge 2:**
- Someone has been releasing caught Pokémon into the [wild area]. The rangers counted over a hundred.
- A gym leader quits and mails her badges back to the League. Her note says she has joined [team].

**After badge 3:**
- [team] posts a list of trainers they want banned from the region. Your name is on it.
- A trainer you met earlier asks you to join [team]. They mean it as a kindness.

**After badge 5:**
- [boss] closes the road to [place] and dares the League to open it again.
- [team] takes over a town hall and starts making the rules there.

**After badge 6:**
- A ranger is hurt clearing one of [team]'s roadblocks. [boss] says it was an accident.
- [boss] warns the League that the next roadblocks will not come down so easily.

**After badge 8:**
- [team] closes every road in the region on the same morning. Only [place] is still open, and [boss] is waiting there.
- [boss] gathers all of [team] at [place] to hold the border. You are going to have to get through them.

## Waking an ancient Pokémon

Team names: The Second Spring, Revenant, The Long Return, Ashgrove, The Old Line

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Wake the ancient Pokémon sleeping under [place], so the region goes back to how it was.
- Drain the flooded [wild area] and build the old town again, even if that floods somewhere else.
- Give the region back to the families who ran it a hundred years ago.

**Method:**
- Their money comes from families who left the region long ago and want it back.
- They dig up old sites and call it a restoration project.
- They hold meetings in every town to win people over.

**After badge 1:**
- [team] opens a dig at [place]. Their permit says they are building a memorial.
- Old maps of the region go missing from three town halls in two weeks.
- [team] offers a [trade] a lot of money for a field nobody else wanted.

**After badge 2:**
- The dig at [place] has reached a stone door, deep underground. [team] posts guards at it.
- [team] members start wearing the old region's colours and using its old town names.

**After badge 3:**
- [boss] tells you the whole plan: open the door at [place] and wake the Pokémon behind it.
- A gym leader admits her grandmother was one of the families [team] wants to bring back.

**After badge 5:**
- The water at [place] starts flowing back toward the dig. Something underground is pulling it.
- Half the [trade]s side with [team]. They miss the region their grandparents knew.

**After badge 6:**
- Something wakes up under [place], and it is angry. Even [team] is scared of it.
- [boss] keeps digging even after the ground starts shaking.

**After badge 8:**
- [boss] is about to open the door at [place] all the way. Stop them before the ancient Pokémon breaks free.
- The ground around [place] is shaking every hour now. [boss] is at the dig, and so is the last of [team].

## Buying up the region

Team names: The Ledger, Ninth Hour, The Long Shift, Sable Company, The Tally

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Buy out every [trade] in the region and run them all as one business.
- Control every road and boat through [place], and charge whatever they like to use them.
- Lend money to so many towns that the League has to do what they say.

**Method:**
- They buy up people’s debts, then demand the money all at once.
- They charge shops to "protect" them, and shops that refuse have bad luck.
- They buy businesses quietly, through a company registered in another region.

**After badge 1:**
- A [trade] sells her shop to a stranger who pays in cash. The stranger works for [team].
- Two shops on the same street are sold in the same week, both to [team].

**After badge 2:**
- A gym leader's Poké Ball supplier now only sells through [team].
- Someone sets fire to the records office at [place]. The town's property papers are gone.

**After badge 3:**
- You find [team]'s account book. On paper, they already own half the region.
- [boss] pays your entry fee at the Pokémon Center before you can, and smiles about it.

**After badge 5:**
- [team] demands that every [trade] pay back their loans on the same morning.
- A harbour town hands its council over to [team] rather than lose its boats.

**After badge 6:**
- [boss] offers you a job that pays more than most people earn in ten years. You say no.
- The League starts an inquiry into [team]. Two witnesses back out before it begins.

**After badge 8:**
- [boss] calls in every debt at once, starting at [place]. Beat [team] there and the region keeps what it owns.
- [boss] is at [place] to sign the papers that make [team] owner of the whole town. You have to stop it.

## Experiments on Pokémon

Team names: Meridian Institute, The Cold Room, Pallas Group, Fieldwork, The Annex

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Prove their theory about the Pokémon of the [wild area], using far more Pokémon than anyone would allow.
- Copy the energy that makes Pokémon at [place] so strong, and sell it.
- Publish their research first, whatever it costs the people and Pokémon living here.

**Method:**
- Three countries pay for their work, and none of them checks what they do.
- They run a research camp that reports to nobody.
- They pay trainers for every Pokémon they catch and hand over.

**After badge 1:**
- A [team] scientist pays a trainer cash for an ordinary Pokémon, then asks for ten more.
- Crates of lab equipment arrive at [place], addressed to a building that does not exist.

**After badge 2:**
- Wild Pokémon near [place] are disappearing. A ranger says a third of them are gone.
- A gym leader lends her strongest Pokémon to [team] for a study. It comes back weak and frightened.

**After badge 3:**
- You sneak into [team]'s camp. Their cages are full of Pokémon from the [wild area].
- [boss] shows you their results and expects you to be impressed. For a moment, you are.

**After badge 5:**
- [team] packs up its camp overnight and sets up again three valleys east.
- A young [team] researcher quits and leaves you her notes, so someone can stop the experiment.

**After badge 6:**
- [team]'s machine at [place] works, and the Pokémon around it are getting sick.
- [boss] refuses to stop the experiment, even though it is hurting the Pokémon.

**After badge 8:**
- [boss] plans to switch the machine at [place] to full power. Get there first and free the Pokémon.
- The Pokémon near [place] are in danger from [team]'s machine. [boss] is guarding it.

## Leaving the League

Team names: The Free Reach, Charter, The Grey Banner, Marchmen, The Standing Vote

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Split the region from the League and run its own gym challenge from [place].
- Make League badges worthless here, so the region has to vote to leave.
- Make the region independent again, the way they say it used to be.

**Method:**
- They run their own gym challenge, with their own badges.
- Gym leaders on their side stop sending results to the League.
- They print a newspaper in every town that tells only their side.

**After badge 1:**
- A gym starts handing out [team] badges as well as League ones.
- [team] posters go up all over [place] overnight, and people leave them up.

**After badge 2:**
- Two gym leaders stop sending their results to the League. Nobody at the League notices for weeks.
- A [trade] says no League official has visited this valley in nine years. He is right.

**After badge 3:**
- [boss] argues with a League official in the town square, and the crowd sides with [boss].
- [team] hands you one of their badges and says you can keep both kinds.

**After badge 5:**
- Half the gyms in the region now accept [team] badges.
- The League threatens to cancel every badge won in the region this year, including yours.

**After badge 6:**
- [boss] calls a vote on leaving the League, and it might pass.
- League officers try to reach [place], but local people block the road.

**After badge 8:**
- The vote is tomorrow. [boss] is at [place], and beating [team] there could change how people vote.
- [team] gathers at [place] the night before the vote. If you beat [boss], the League keeps the region.

## Controlling the weather

Team names: Weatherwise, The Turning, Stormcalled, The Dry Season, Anticyclone

**Goal (one is picked):**
- Keep the rain over [place] until the [wild area] recovers, even if other towns go dry.
- End their drought by taking rain from the next region over.
- Prove they can control the region's seasons.

**Method:**
- They built a weather machine that most people have only heard rumours about.
- They make weather Pokémon work in shifts until they are exhausted.
- Desperate farmers pay for their work.

**After badge 1:**
- It has rained on [place] for eleven days, and nowhere else at all.
- A [trade] says the wind changed direction overnight, which never happens this time of year.

**After badge 2:**
- A valley two ranges over has its first failed harvest in forty years. It has not rained there in weeks.
- [team] is buying every weather Pokémon it can find, at any price.

**After badge 3:**
- You find [team]'s weather machine. It is smaller than the rumours, and the Pokémon powering it look tired.
- [boss] shows you the rainfall records and asks what you would have done.

**After badge 5:**
- A flood hits [place] in the middle of the dry season.
- The [trade]s argue. Half want [team] stopped, and half want the rain sent their way next.

**After badge 6:**
- The weather Pokémon powering the machine are collapsing, and [team] keeps it running.
- [boss] admits the machine cannot be switched off, only pointed somewhere else.

**After badge 8:**
- [boss] plans to aim the machine at the whole region from [place]. Stop it before the storms start.
- Storm clouds are gathering over [place], where [boss] has the machine. This is your chance to end it.

- Beats you to every gym for the first half of the journey, then stops competing entirely.
- Is here because their older sibling tried this gym challenge and gave up.
- Thinks [boss] is right, and says so in public before it is safe to.

- Wants to run the gym in [place] one day, and takes every loss personally.
- Left the region as a kid and came back to prove that leaving was worth it.
